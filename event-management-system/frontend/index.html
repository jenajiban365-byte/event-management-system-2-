GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before you book.

Do I need an account to browse events?

No — anyone can browse and search events without signing up. You'll only need an account to actually book a spot, so you can manage and cancel your bookings later.

Is EventHub free to use?

Yes, creating an account and booking events through EventHub is completely free.

What happens if an event is full?

You can join the waitlist. If someone with a confirmed spot cancels, the next person on the waitlist is automatically confirmed and notified by email — no need to keep checking back.

Can I cancel a booking after I've made it?

Yes, anytime from your "My Bookings" page. Cancelling immediately frees up your spot for the next person on the waitlist.

How do I sign in?

Use your email and password, or sign in instantly with your Google account — whichever's easier for you.
